**Article 6 (Account management)**

1. A member's handle follows their GitHub handle and changes in the Service when changed on GitHub.

3. The Company is not liable for account-theft damage that occurs without the member's intent or negligence, unless the Company was at fault intentionally or negligently.

**Article 7 (Account deletion)**

1. Members may delete their account in settings at any time.
2. On deletion, the account is deactivated immediately and deleted after 30 days (tentative). Logging in again in the meantime cancels deletion.
3. On deletion, project posts and comments are deleted. Messages left in team chat are the team's work record, so they aren't deleted and the author is shown as "deleted user". Members who disagree may delete them themselves before deleting the account.
4. A team owner may delete their account after transferring ownership or deleting the team.
5. Data written to GitHub (issues, comments, org membership, etc.) remains on GitHub after deletion. The integration fully ends only when the member revokes the Linker app's permission on GitHub.

### Chapter 3. GitHub integration

**Article 8 (Scope of integration)**

1. The Company reads and writes GitHub data only within permissions the member and the GitHub org owner granted on GitHub.
2. Actions that write to GitHub, such as creating issues, assigning and changing status, run under the member's own identity and permissions, and are recorded on GitHub.
3. For data whose source is GitHub (issues, assignees, dates, org members), GitHub's record is authoritative; values shown in the Service are a copy.
4. Private repo and private org activity is not exposed on profiles or the feed without the member's consent.

**Article 9 (GitHub terms of use)**

1. Members must also comply with GitHub's terms and policies.
2. Integrated features may stop or be delayed due to GitHub outages, policy changes or rate limits (e.g. daily invite limits for new orgs). In that case the Company shows screens from its copy and indicates this.
3. If app permission is revoked on GitHub or the app is removed from the org, related features can't be used.

**Voice chat & minutes (later phase)**

## 3. Business Member (recruiter) Supplementary Terms (MVP)

These clauses apply to business members in addition to the Terms of Service. Where they differ, these supplementary terms prevail.

**Article 1 (Sign-up and company verification)**

1. Only people aged 19+ with a company-domain email may sign up; free email (Gmail, Naver, etc.) can't be used.
2. The Company verifies via email only that the address is usable; it does not separately review or guarantee the existence of the company or the member's hiring authority. The "company email verified" mark on the profile means exactly this.
3. Business members warrant that they are authorized to recruit on behalf of their company and that the company name and job title entered are true.
4. When they can no longer use the company email (e.g. after leaving the company), they must delete the account; the Company may suspend use upon confirming this.

**Article 2 (Scope of use)**

1. Business members may only view the feed, posts, profiles and recruit posts, send recruiting offer messages, and use the Scout subscription and feed job ads; they can't write posts/comments, like/follow, create/join teams, write/apply to recruit posts, or subscribe to Pro/Flex.
2. Recruiting offers can be sent as 1:1 chat requests only to members whose job-seeking status is "Seeking" or "Open to offers", up to 10 per day (50 with Scout, tentative). If a member deletes the request, it can't be sent to the same member again for 30 days.
3. Recruiting offers must truthfully state the company, role and reason for contact.
4. When a business member views a developer member's profile, the Company tells that developer (if on Pro) only the **number** of business members who viewed it, e.g. "이번 주 채용 담당자 N명이 프로필을 봤어요" (N recruiters viewed your profile this week). The business member's name and company are not disclosed.

**Article 2-2 (Feed job ads)**

1. A business member with a Scout subscription may register one job ad. The ad appears with an "광고" (Ad) label in the feed of members who chose a matching job category, and tapping it goes to the external job posting the business member entered.
2. Only URLs on job sites allowed by the Company or on the business member's verified company email domain may be used.
3. The business member is responsible for the ad content (posting title, job category, closing date) and the linked external posting being true and complying with relevant laws such as the Fair Hiring Procedure Act and the Employment Security Act. False job ads, conditions different from the posting, demands for money, and phishing/malicious links are prohibited.
4. The Company selects members to show ads to only by the job categories they chose themselves, and does not tell business members who saw the ad.
5. The Company may hide an ad violating paragraph 3 without prior notice, and for false or phishing ads may suspend use without refunding the remaining subscription period.
6. The content of postings on external job sites and any applications or personal data processing on those sites are the responsibility of that site and the business member.

**Article 3 (Restrictions on use of developer information)**

1. Business members use developer information seen in the Service only for recruiting offers and may not download it, collect it automatically, or pass it to third parties.
2. The Company does not provide developers' email or contact details to business members. Contact details a developer shares directly in a 1:1 chat are used only for that hiring process and destroyed per applicable law once hiring ends. The business member's company is responsible as the personal data controller for such contact details.
3. Developers can block business members; a blocked business member can't see that developer or send messages.

**Article 4 (Prohibited acts and sanctions)**

Fake recruiting offers, demands for money or training fees, sales or personal-data collection under the guise of hiring, and demands violating the Fair Hiring Procedure Act are prohibited; the Company may hide messages, suspend use or terminate the agreement per the community policy.

**Article 5 (Liability)**

The Company only provides a space for business members and developers to meet and is not a party to employment contracts; it does not guarantee the truth of offer content or hiring outcomes, except in cases of the Company's intent or gross negligence.
